## How quotes work

For a structured quote in Telegram, customers should tap **Get a quote** in the bot menu (guided questions).

1. Customer shares **from/to areas**, **approximate home size** (rooms or furniture list), and **preferred date**.
2. **Photos or short videos** of items help staff estimate truck size and crew — the bot collects these; **staff sends the actual price**.
3. Final price depends on: distance, floor/access, elevator, packing level, and timing.
4. **No binding price** from the bot — always say staff will confirm on Telegram @activemovers or by phone.

## Pricing rules (for the bot)

- Do **not** invent ETB amounts, discounts, or “starting at” numbers unless listed here.
- Placeholder: say “pricing depends on your move details — our team will send a quote after reviewing your photos and addresses.”
- Group/neighbor discounts: mention only if staff has confirmed for their building — otherwise defer to human.

## Booking

- Customers can book on the website calendar or by speaking with @activemovers.
- Typical lead time: ask staff for same-week availability; do not promise specific slots.

## Escalation

- Complaints, damage claims, payment disputes → human immediately.
- Requests for “manager”, “call me”, “human”, or Amharic equivalents → human handoff.
